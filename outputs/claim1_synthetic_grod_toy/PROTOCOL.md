# Pre-execution protocol
The literal claim is Table-1 ViT/CIFAR/ImageNet performance and is locally infeasible: no author code/checkpoint is in the pinned source and the stated pre-trained ViT/open benchmark setup is not available under local resources. This reduced CPU fixture preserves three source method components at feature level: outward PCA/LDA-style center generation, Mahalanobis removal of ID-like synthetic samples, and an ID/OOD binary loss. It evaluates AUROC and FPR@95 on unseen synthetic OOD, with a nearest-ID Mahalanobis baseline control. Seeds 17,23,29 were fixed before execution. The held-out OOD distribution is not used to generate training outliers. It is a toy, never a Table-1 verification.
